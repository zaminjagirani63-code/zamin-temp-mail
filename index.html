Frequently Asked Questions

Useful information about temporary email

What is a temporary email?

A temporary email is a disposable email address that you can use to receive messages without sharing your personal email address.

How do I receive an OTP?

Copy your generated email, use it on the website requesting verification, then refresh your inbox to check for new messages.

Can I use this inbox for private accounts?

No. Temporary inboxes may be publicly accessible to anyone who knows the address. Avoid using them for banking, personal accounts, or sensitive information.

Why is my inbox empty?

The sender may not have sent the message yet, or the service may not support that email provider. Wait a little and refresh your inbox.
